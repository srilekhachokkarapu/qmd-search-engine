# Introduction

Medicines are substances used to prevent,
manage or treat health conditions.
Their use should follow appropriate medical guidance.

## Paracetamol

Paracetamol is a medicine commonly used
for pain relief and fever reduction.

### Uses

- Relief from mild to moderate pain.
- Reduction of fever.

### Safety Information

Excessive paracetamol can cause serious liver damage.
Follow the prescribed or package dosage instructions.
Check other medicines for overlapping ingredients.

## Ibuprofen

Ibuprofen is a non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drug.

### Uses

- Relief from certain types of pain.
- Reduction of inflammation.
- Reduction of fever.

### Safety Information

Ibuprofen may cause stomach irritation and other
side effects. It may not be suitable for everyone.
Seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
