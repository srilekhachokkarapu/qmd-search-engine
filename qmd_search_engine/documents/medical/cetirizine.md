# Cetirizine

## 1. Overview

Cetirizine is a second-generation H1 antihistamine used to relieve symptoms of allergic conditions.

It is commonly used for allergic rhinitis and urticaria (hives).

It is generally less sedating than older antihistamines, but it can still cause sleepiness in some people.

This document is general educational information, not personal medical advice.

## 2. Drug Classification

- Therapeutic class: Antihistamine; antiallergic
- Pharmacological class: Second-generation histamine H1-receptor antagonist
- Generic name: Cetirizine

## 3. Common Uses

Cetirizine may be used to relieve symptoms such as:

- Sneezing
- Runny or itchy nose
- Itchy or watery eyes
- Itching associated with allergic conditions
- Hives (urticaria) and associated itching

It can relieve symptoms but does not remove the underlying allergy trigger.

Sudden severe allergic reactions (anaphylaxis) require emergency treatment. Cetirizine is not a substitute for emergency care or prescribed adrenaline/epinephrine.

## 4. Mechanism of Action

Histamine is a chemical released during allergic responses.

It binds to H1 receptors and contributes to itching, sneezing, runny nose and hives.

Cetirizine selectively blocks peripheral H1 receptors, reducing the effects of histamine.

## 5. Available Dosage Forms

Depending on country and manufacturer, cetirizine may be available as:

- Tablets
- Oral solution or syrup
- Chewable tablets
- Other locally approved formulations

Strengths and age restrictions vary.

Children's products require particular attention to age, formulation and measuring instructions.

## 6. Use and Administration Principles

- Follow the exact product label or prescription.
- Do not exceed the labelled or prescribed amount.
- Use the supplied measuring device for liquid formulations.
- Do not combine with another antihistamine unless recommended by a clinician or pharmacist.
- Avoid driving or operating machinery if you feel sleepy, dizzy or less alert.
- Ask about dose suitability if you have kidney disease.

This reference does not give a universal dose because product strengths, age approvals and kidney-related adjustments differ.

## 7. Common Side Effects

Possible side effects include:

- Sleepiness or tiredness
- Headache
- Dry mouth
- Nausea
- Dizziness
- Sometimes stomach discomfort

Although cetirizine is considered less sedating than some older antihistamines, drowsiness can still occur.

## 8. Serious Risks and Warnings

Serious reactions are uncommon but can include:

- Severe allergic reaction to the medicine
- Significant confusion or unusual sedation
- Difficulty passing urine in susceptible individuals

Swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat, breathing difficulty, collapse or a rapidly worsening allergic reaction requires emergency care.

## 9. Contraindications and Precautions

Seek professional advice before use if you:

- Have had an allergic reaction to cetirizine, hydroxyzine or product ingredients
- Have kidney impairment or kidney failure
- Have epilepsy or a seizure disorder
- Have difficulty urinating or urinary retention
- Are pregnant or breastfeeding
- Are due to undergo allergy testing, because antihistamines may affect test results

Follow the age limits on the exact product.

## 10. Drug and Substance Interactions

Cetirizine may increase drowsiness when combined with:

- Alcohol
- Sedatives or sleeping medicines
- Opioid pain medicines
- Other medicines that cause sleepiness

Other medicines with anticholinergic or urinary-retention effects may require review.

Tell the pharmacist about prescription medicines, non-prescription products and supplements.

## 11. Missed Dose

Follow the product leaflet or pharmacist's instructions.

Do not take a double dose to make up for a missed dose.

If cetirizine is being used only when symptoms occur, follow the product's directions for as-needed use.

## 12. Storage and Handling

- Keep in the original labelled package.
- Follow the storage temperature and handling instructions.
- Keep out of reach and sight of children.
- Use the supplied measuring device for liquid medicine.
- Do not use after the expiry date.

## 13. Patient Education Checklist

Before using cetirizine:

1. Confirm the correct formulation and age suitability.
2. Check for kidney disease or urinary-retention problems.
3. Be alert to sleepiness and avoid driving if affected.
4. Avoid combining it with alcohol or sedating medicines without advice.
5. Understand that it does not replace emergency treatment for anaphylaxis.

## 14. When to Seek Medical Help

Seek emergency help for breathing difficulty, throat or tongue swelling, collapse or signs of anaphylaxis.

Contact a clinician if symptoms are persistent, worsening or accompanied by troublesome side effects or difficulty urinating.

## 15. Key Facts for Document Search

- Cetirizine is a second-generation H1 antihistamine.
- It is used for allergic rhinitis symptoms and hives.
- It blocks histamine H1 receptors.
- Sleepiness and tiredness are possible.
- Kidney impairment may affect suitability or dosing.
- It is not emergency treatment for anaphylaxis.
